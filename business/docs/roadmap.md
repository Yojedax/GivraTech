### What Success Looks Like

By end of Month 1:
- 1–2 paid audits closed OR committed
- 3–5 discovery calls held, at least 2 leading to proposals
- At least one case study in hand (even if client is anonymous)
- Delivery playbook refined (you know exactly what works)
- Agents are 40–50% reliable (some manual tweaking needed, but mostly automated)

### What Failure Looks Like

- Fewer than 10% reply rate on outreach (bad targeting or messaging)
- No discovery calls booked (objection: "I need to see proof of concept first")
- Delivery takes >12 days (process is too slow to scale)
- Audit findings are inconclusive (data quality or analysis issue)
- Zero testimonials or internal confidence in the offer

**If any of these occur:** Pause outreach, do a postmortem, pivot offer/positioning, go back to 5–10 targeted warm outreach before resuming at scale.

### Productization Decision (Days 75–80)

**Decision Point:** Should Givra Tech become a SaaS product or stay high-touch consulting?

**SaaS Path:**
- Build self-serve tool: customers upload data, get segments + dashboard
- Pricing: $99–499/month
- Pros: Passive income, unlimited scale, lower delivery burden
- Cons: 3–6 months to build, support burden, lower margins initially

**Consulting Path:**
- Stay high-touch (audits, implementations, retainers)
- Hire support to handle delivery
- Pricing: $500–10K per engagement
- Pros: Higher margins, faster revenue, personal relationships
- Cons: Time-bound, hard to scale beyond personal bandwidth

**Recommendation for Month 3:** Stay consulting. Build to $10K MRR, then decide. SaaS is a separate company; don't distract yourself now. If you hit $10K MRR by Month 6 consistently, then revisit SaaS.

## THE PATH TO $10K MRR

**MRR = Monthly Recurring Revenue. Retainers are the path there.**

### Current Model (High-Touch, Project-Based)

**Month 3 example:**
- 6 audits @ $1,500 avg = $9,000
- 2 implementations @ $4,000 avg = $8,000
- 1 retainer @ $2,000/month = $2,000
- **One-time revenue: $17,000 + MRR: $2,000 = $19,000 total**

**Problem:** This is not sustainable at $10K MRR without hiring. Each audit + implementation takes time. To hit $10K MRR recurring, you need:

**Option A: 5–7 retainer clients @ $1,500–2,000 each**
- 5 retainers @ $2,000 = $10K MRR
- Month to build this: Month 4–5
- Requires: 15–20 audit→implementation→retainer conversions

**Option B: Hybrid (Audits + Implementations + Retainers)**
- 2 retainers @ $1,500 = $3,000 MRR
- 1 implementation/month @ $3,500 = $3,500 MRR
- 2 audits/month @ $1,500 = $3,000 MRR
- **Total: $9,500 MRR (mix of one-time and recurring)**

**Option C: ProductionICS / Lighter Touch**
- Build template-based audits (half-day to deliver) = $1,000 × 5 = $5,000 MRR
- Retainers @ $1,500 × 3 = $4,500 MRR
- **Total: $9,500 MRR (lower touch, higher volume)**

### Timeline to $10K MRR

**Realistic path (conservative):**
- Month 3: $2,000 MRR (1 retainer) + $8,500 one-time = $10,500 total
- Month 4: $6,000 MRR (3 retainers) + $6,000 one-time = $12,000 total
- Month 5: $10,000+ MRR (5–7 retainers) + lower one-time work

**Conditions:**
1. Audit→Implementation→Retainer conversion rate stays 30–40%
2. You maintain outreach (200+ messages/month) to generate leads
3. Sales cycle stays 10–14 days
4. Delivery remains high-quality (no churn)

### What Could Go Wrong

- **Delivery burden becomes unsustainable.** You're doing 6 audits + 2 implementations/month by Month 3. That's 40–50 hours/week of delivery work + ongoing outreach. Solution: Hire contractor (part-time analyst, $1,500–3,000/month) to handle data work while you do strategy.

- **Retainer churn is high.** Clients sign, but leave after Month 2 because they don't see ROI. Solution: Build stronger onboarding, clearer success metrics, monthly strategy calls (not just data refreshes).

- **Outreach doesn't scale.** You run out of warm leads and cold outreach gets worse. Solution: Hire VA to handle outreach by Month 4 ($1,500–2,000/month), or fully automate with agents.

## DECISION GATES (Go/No-Go Checkpoints)

**End of Month 1 (Day 30):**
- Did we close at least 1 audit? If NO: Pivot messaging or positioning and try again.
- Did we book 3+ discovery calls? If NO: Improve targeting or offer clarity.
- Do we have 1 case study or testimonial? If NO: Deliver perfection to first audit client and ask for it.
- **Decision:** Proceed to Month 2 scaling, OR pivot before ramping up.

**End of Month 2 (Day 60):**
- Did we close 2+ audits at full price? If NO: Price is too high or messaging is weak. Lower price or refocus on pain points.
- Did we complete 1 implementation? If NO: Audit clients aren't ready for upsell. Improve audit quality or adjust upsell timing.
- Do we have 3+ case studies? If NO: Double down on case study documentation and content.
- **Decision:** Scale to 6 audits/month in Month 3, OR optimize sales process before scaling.

**End of Month 3 (Day 90):**
- Did we close 4+ audits? If NO: Outreach or conversion issue. Audit should be repeatable by now.
- Do we have 1+ retainer clients? If NO: Implementations aren't strong enough for upsell. Improve delivery.
- Are we tracking toward $10K MRR by Month 6? If NO: Which offer tier is under-performing? Pivot or kill it.
- **Decision:** Hire (VA for outreach, contractor for delivery) to scale, OR stay solo and focus on higher-margin retainer deals.

## SUMMARY: The Next 90 Days

**Month 1 (Validation):** Prove the audit works. Deliver perfection. Collect proof.

**Month 2 (Traction):** Close 2–4 audits. Launch implementation upsell. Build content flywheel.

**Month 3 (Scale):** Close 4–6 audits. Complete 1–2 implementations. Sign first retainer(s). Make hiring decision.

**By Day 90:** $10K–30K revenue, 1–2 retainer clients, 5+ case studies, clear path to $10K MRR.

**Success looks like:** You're booked out 2–3 weeks in advance, turning down leads, and deciding whether to hire or raise prices.

**Failure looks like:** Fewer than 5 discovery calls/month, no repeat/referral business, burning out, questioning the offer.

**If failure happens:** Go back to Month 1. Pick one thing that didn't work (offer, positioning, targeting, delivery) and fix it. Don't pivot everything at once.
