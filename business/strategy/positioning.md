## 3. Primary Niche Choice: E-commerce & Retail (LatAm)

### Full Justification

**We are positioning Givra Tech exclusively in E-commerce & Retail in LatAm for the first 12 months.**

**Rationale:**

1. **Founder-Market Fit is Exceptional**
   - Native Spanish speaker. Argentine operator. Understands local e-commerce dynamics, payment methods, regulatory environment.
   - RFM segmentation is core expertise. This is the exact technique needed by the target market.
   - Can build local reputation and word-of-mouth fast. Network effects in Argentine tech community.

2. **Market is Massive and Underserved**
   - 2.5M+ e-commerce businesses across LatAm. Growth of 15%+ YoY.
   - Most are using generic analytics (Google Analytics, Shopify dashboard). Almost zero have sophisticated customer segmentation or churn prediction.
   - Competitive intensity is low: No local specialists doing RFM + churn + address normalization.

3. **Pain is Urgent and Measurable**
   - Average cart abandonment: 70%+
   - Average customer churn: 40-50% annually
   - Average AOV increases: 15-25% if churn is addressed and segments are known
   - Problem translates directly to revenue loss—easy to quantify and justify budget.

4. **Sales Cycle is Short (1-3 weeks)**
   - Decision-makers are e-commerce owners/CMOs/heads of growth.
   - Budgets are tight but flexible for proven ROI.
   - Willingness to experiment is high (bootstrapped founders testing new channels constantly).
   - Entry price ($500-1.5K) is easy to approve quickly.

5. **Repeatability is Very High**
   - Every Shopify, WooCommerce, Tienda Nube, or custom-built e-commerce system has customer transaction data.
   - Data structure is standardizable: Transactions, dates, values, customer IDs.
   - Audit process can be productized within 2-3 engagements.
   - Can scale from 1 founder to 2-3 contractors without losing quality.

6. **Pricing is Sustainable**
   - Entry audit: $500-1.5K. Low enough to be a no-brainer for growth-focused e-commerce businesses.
   - Implementation: $3.5K-8K. Value justifiable by revenue opportunity identified in audit.
   - Retainer: $1.2K-2K/month. Sticky, recurring revenue from early wins.
   - Total first-year potential: $6K-12K per customer (audit + 6 months of retainer).

7. **Pathway to Scale is Clear**
   - Start with audit productization. Get 5-10 paying clients by end of Q2 2026.
   - Build a portfolio of case studies (confidential but quantified: e.g., "Helped 3 e-commerce brands reduce churn by 12% in 90 days, driving $150K+ incremental revenue").
   - Move to productized implementation (pre-built playbooks, templated dashboards, standardized training).
   - By Q4 2026, introduce SaaS dashboard for ongoing RFM monitoring and churn alerts (subscription: $500-1.5K/month).
   - Hire contractor RFM specialists to deliver audits faster.

## 6. Pricing Rationale

### Why These Prices Work

**AI Growth Audit: $500-1,500**
- Entry point must be low enough to feel like a no-brainer for a bootstrapped e-commerce founder.
- $500 is risk-free for a $20K/month business (2.5% of monthly revenue).
- $1,500 is justified only if we know the prospect has higher revenue ($100K+/month).
- We'll anchor at $1,000 as the standard, with flexibility down to $500 for referrals/case studies.
- At $1,000 × 10 customers = $10,000 by Q2. Sustainable for initial growth phase.

**Growth Implementation: $3,500-8,000**
- Justifiable only if the audit shows clear revenue opportunity ($100K+ in identified upside).
- Positioned as "70-80% of the revenue opportunity you're leaving on the table."
- If audit identifies $200K in potential, $5,000 implementation is an ROI of 40:1 (breakeven in 9 days of execution).
- Smaller businesses ($20-50K/month revenue): $3,500
- Mid-market ($50K-300K/month): $5,000-8,000
- High-confidence conversions (from audit leads): 40-50% closing rate expected.

**Data Intelligence Retainer: $1,200-2,000/month**
- Recurring revenue model. Sticky. Customer pays because they see results.
- Positioned as "insurance against churn + competitive advantage."
- $1,500/month is only $18K/year—trivial compared to the revenue it protects.
- Can upsell from audit → implementation → retainer (3-month implementation + 12-month retainer = $18K-26K lifetime value).
- Target 10-15 retainer customers by end of 2026 = $15K-30K/month recurring revenue.

### Competitive Positioning
- Generic analytics consultants: $200-500 for a one-off analysis. We're 2-3x their price but with more specificity.
- Big-name agencies: $10K-50K for vague "growth strategy." We're underpriced but vastly more actionable.
- DIY tools (Shopify app, GA4 dashboards): Free-$300/month. We're the human layer that interprets and acts.

### Volume-Based Adjustments
- Referral discount: 10% off audit ($900 instead of $1,000)
- Cohort discount: 15% off if they bring 2 other e-commerce friends
- Nonprofit/SMB rate: $500 flat (build goodwill, case studies)
- Enterprise: $2K-3K audit + negotiate on implementation
