# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Givra Tech

---

## Primary ICP: E-commerce & Retail (LatAm)

### Company Profile

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Company Size** | 5-100 employees (core team) |
| **Annual Revenue** | $250K-5M USD (measurable e-commerce businesses) |
| **Monthly Customer Database** | 500-50,000 active customers |
| **Monthly Transactions** | 50-10,000+ transactions/month |
| **Avg Customer Base Growth** | 5-20% YoY (actively growing businesses) |
| **Customer Lifetime Value** | $20-500+ USD (repeatable business model) |
| **Monthly Revenue** | $10K-300K+ USD |
| **Stage** | Bootstrapped to Series A (venture-backed) |

**Rationale:** Below 500 customers = too early; no data patterns. Above 50K customers = likely has in-house analytics already. Monthly revenue $10K minimum = budget for services. $5M+ annual = typically has data team.

### Pain Points (Specific, Not Generic)

**Primary Pains:**

1. **Churn Blindness**
   - "I know customers disappear, but I don't know why or which ones matter."
   - "I have a customer list, but 50% haven't ordered in 6 months."
   - "We spend on acquisition but lose customers faster than we gain them."
   - No early warning system for customer dropout.

2. **Revenue Concentration Risk**
   - "My top 10 customers are 60% of revenue—if they leave, we're in trouble."
   - "I don't know which customers to prioritize for retention vs. acquisition."
   - No segmentation; everyone is treated equally.

3. **Average Order Value Stagnation**
   - "AOV has been flat for 18 months even though we're adding customers."
   - "I don't know what products to recommend to each customer type."
   - No cross-sell or upsell strategy; leaving money on table.

4. **Marketing Budget Waste**
   - "We send the same promotions to everyone—doesn't work for new customers or repeat whales."
   - "Email open rates are 5-10%; we don't segment by customer lifecycle."
   - "We're spending acquisition budget on retention—it's backwards but we don't know how to fix it."

5. **Data Scattered Across Tools**
   - "Transactions are in Shopify, email engagement is in Klaviyo, we have ad spend in Meta Ads, but they're not connected."
   - "I export data to Excel every month and manually create pivot tables."
   - "We have data, but no confidence it's accurate."

6. **Growth Plateau**
   - "We grew 30% last year, but growth is slowing now."
   - "We're adding more traffic but not more revenue—something's wrong with conversion or retention."
   - "I think there's revenue hiding in our existing customer base, but I don't know where."

### Triggers That Make Them Ready to Buy

**Situational Triggers:**
- Raised funding or brought in new investment (planning horizon expanded)
- Hired a CMO or Head of Growth (new leader wants to prove value fast)
- Churn rate visibly increased (urgency spike)
- Competitors are growing faster (FOMO)
- Year-end planning (Q1 budgets being allocated now)
- Ad costs increasing (CAC rising, need to optimize LTV)
- Failed past analytics project (receptive to new approach)
- Seasonal business cycle (pre-holiday planning for e-commerce)

**Timeline Triggers:**
- "We're launching a new product line / expansion" (need segmentation to target right audience)
- "We're planning a re-branding / positioning refresh" (need customer insights)
- "We're evaluating a new email/CRM platform" (right time to audit current database)
- "We're hiring a new growth person" (budget allocated)

**Conversation Triggers:**
- They mention "customer segments" or "cohorts" or "churn" unprompted
- They ask about "marketing automation" or "personalization"
- They say "We need better data" or "Our team is drowning in spreadsheets"
- They ask "How do I reduce churn?" or "What's our actual repeat rate?"

### Decision Maker Profile

**Primary Contact (Best First Reach):**
- **Title:** Head of Growth, Growth Manager, CMO, VP of E-commerce
- **Motivation:** Quick wins. Proven ROI. Data to justify budget allocation.
- **Budget Authority:** $500-3K (yes, they can sign off); $5K+ (requires approval from founder/CFO)
- **Decision Speed:** 1-2 weeks if problem is acute
- **Skepticism Level:** Medium (wants proof but willing to experiment)

**Secondary Contact (Influencer):**
- **Title:** Founder, CEO
- **Motivation:** Revenue growth. Reducing risk. Competitive advantage.
- **Budget Authority:** Yes (final authority on implementation budget)
- **Decision Speed:** Slower (wants long-term view) but decisive once convinced

**Tertiary Contact (Avoid):**
- **Title:** Data Analyst, BI Developer
- **Why Avoid:** Will want to build it themselves; philosophically opposed to outsourcing; will scrutinize methodology exhaustively

## Qualification Questions (Discovery Call)

Use these 7 questions in discovery calls to confirm fit and identify urgency:

### Question 1: Understand Current State
**"Walk me through your customer base. How many active customers do you have, and what's your repeat purchase rate?"**
- Listen for: Specific numbers (good signal) vs. "I dunno, thousands?" (bad signal)
- What you're assessing: Do they have data? Is it organized?
- Disqualify if: <500 customers or no idea what repeat rate is

### Question 2: Identify Pain
**"When you think about customer churn—customers who disappear—what's your sense of how much you lose annually, and does that concern you?"**
- Listen for: Acknowledgment of the problem; admission of not knowing the exact number
- What you're assessing: Do they feel the pain? Are they motivated to solve it?
- Disqualify if: "We don't have churn" (they don't understand the problem) or extreme apathy

### Question 3: Gauge Revenue Impact
**"If you could reduce customer churn by 15% without increasing acquisition spend, what would that mean for your revenue?"**
- Listen for: Quick math or enthusiasm (e.g., "That'd be six figures for us")
- What you're assessing: Can they connect data insights to revenue? Do they see value?
- Qualify if: They can articulate the revenue impact clearly

### Question 4: Assess Current Approach
**"Are you currently doing any customer segmentation or RFM analysis? Or is it more of a one-size-fits-all approach?"**
- Listen for: "We haven't thought about it" or "We've tried but it's messy"
- What you're assessing: Are they unsophisticated (good for us) or already have solution (bad)?
- Disqualify if: "We have a whole data science team and use Palantir" (too advanced)

### Question 5: Understand Urgency
**"If you had a clear roadmap showing exactly which customers to focus on and what actions to take in the next 90 days, would you implement it?"**
- Listen for: "Absolutely" or "Probably" (good) vs. "Maybe" (bad)
- What you're assessing: Is this academic exercise or genuine need?
- Qualify if: Clear yes; they describe themselves as action-oriented

### Question 6: Identify Budget & Authority
**"If we found $50K-$300K in revenue opportunity hiding in your existing customer base, what's the process for approving a project like this? And who would make the decision?"**
- Listen for: "I can decide" (founder/CMO) or "I'd need to talk to X" (some friction, acceptable)
- What you're assessing: Can they buy? Is there budget?
- Disqualify if: "I'd need approval from 5 people and our board" (too slow)

### Question 7: Close & Commitment
**"Based on what we've talked about, does it make sense to do a 7-day diagnostic audit to get specific numbers and a roadmap? If we find real opportunities, could you see moving on implementation?"**
- Listen for: "Yes, let's do it" or "I'd like to discuss with my team"
- What you're assessing: Are they ready to move forward?
- Next step if yes: Send audit proposal
- Next step if hesitant: Ask what would make them ready; offer to send case study

```
INBOUND LEAD → Q1: Do you have 500+ customers and organized data?
   ├─ No → PASS (too early)
   └─ Yes → Q2: Are you losing customers you'd like to keep?
       ├─ No → PASS (no pain)
       └─ Yes → Q3: If you could reduce churn 15%, would you take action?
           ├─ No → PASS (not motivated)
           └─ Yes → Q4: Do you have budget to invest in growth?
               ├─ No → ADD TO NURTURE (revisit in 6 months)
               └─ Yes → Q5: Can you decide on a $1K diagnostic this week?
                   ├─ No → ASK: What would help you move faster? / SEND CASE STUDY
                   └─ Yes → SEND AUDIT PROPOSAL → QUALIFIED LEAD
```
