# AI Growth Audit: Complete Offer Specification

---

## 1. Product Overview

The **AI Growth Audit** is a 7-day diagnostic engagement that analyzes your customer database, identifies hidden revenue opportunities, and provides a prioritized 90-day action roadmap. Using advanced customer segmentation (RFM analysis), churn prediction, and revenue opportunity identification, we deliver a clear, data-driven strategy you can execute immediately. You get a comprehensive PDF report, segmented customer lists in Excel, a video walkthrough, and a live strategy call to ensure you understand every recommendation.

## 2. What's Included (Detailed List)

### 2.1 Customer Database Quality Audit
- **What we do:** Analyze your customer data for completeness, consistency, and usability
- **Deliverable:** Quality scorecard showing data completeness % by field (email, phone, address, purchase history)
- **Detail level:** Identify missing/duplicate records, data type mismatches, suspicious entries
- **Use case:** Understand if your data can support segmentation and personalization—or if cleanup is needed first
- **In report:** 1-2 pages with findings and remediation suggestions

### 2.2 RFM Segmentation with Segment Breakdown
- **What we do:** Build RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segments based on transaction history
- **Segmentation model:**
  - Recency: Days since last purchase (0-30, 31-90, 91-180, 180+)
  - Frequency: # of purchases in 12 months (1, 2-5, 6-10, 11+)
  - Monetary: Total $ spent in 12 months (quartiles: low/mid-low/mid-high/high)
  - Result: 8-16 customer segments with clear behavioral profiles
- **Deliverable:**
  - Table showing: Segment name, # of customers, % of base, avg revenue per segment, repeat rate, avg order value
  - Customer list (Excel) with segment assignments
- **Segment profiles:** For each segment, narrative describing: who they are, their value, their behavior patterns
- **In report:** 3-4 pages; in Excel: Full customer list with segment labels

### 2.3 Churn Risk Detection (Top At-Risk Customers Identified)
- **What we do:** Identify customers with early churn signals—likely to leave in next 90 days
- **Methodology:**
  - Analyze purchase frequency decline: e.g., "Bought monthly for 6 months, then nothing for 45 days"
  - Track engagement fade: Time since last order increasing + order frequency decreasing
  - Calculate churn risk score (1-100): Low risk (1-30), Medium (31-70), High (71-100)
- **Deliverable:**
  - Top 100 at-risk customers with: Name, email, phone, last purchase date, churn risk score, reactivation revenue if recovered
  - Cohort analysis: "X% of your $Y revenue is at risk of churn in next 90 days"
  - Segmented list: Which segments have highest churn risk
- **In report:** 2-3 pages with tables; in Excel: Full at-risk customer list with risk scores

### 2.4 Address/Geolocation Quality Audit (If Applicable)
- **What we do:** Analyze address data for standardization, completeness, and location intelligence
- **Deliverable:**
  - Address completeness score (% with valid address)
  - Standardization issues identified (e.g., "123 Street" vs "123 St" vs "123 S")
  - Geographic concentration: Which regions/cities drive revenue
  - Geographic gaps: Regions underserved or underperforming
- **Use case:** Inform regional marketing strategy; identify underserved territories; prepare for address-based campaigns
- **In report:** 1-2 pages; in Excel: Customers by city/region with revenue breakdown
- **Note:** Included if address data is available; skipped if you're primarily B2B or don't have addresses

### 2.5 Top 5 Revenue Opportunities (Actionable, Quantified)
- **What we do:** Identify the 5 highest-impact revenue moves you can make in 90 days
- **Methodology:** Combine segment analysis, churn risk, AOV trends, and cohort behavior
- **Examples of opportunities:**
  1. "Reactivation campaign for high-value customers at churn risk: 200 customers, $150K potential revenue if 20% respond"
  2. "Upsell campaign to mid-tier customers: 500 customers, $30K revenue if AOV increases 15%"
  3. "Retention campaign for new customers: 400 customers in first 30 days, $25K revenue if repeat rate improves 10%"
  4. "Cross-sell campaign: Customers buying product A rarely buy product B; $40K revenue if 5% cross-sell"
  5. "Referral program targeting top customers: 50 VIP customers could refer 10 each; $50K potential"
- **Deliverable:** Table with: Opportunity name, target segment, # of customers, revenue potential, success metric, why it matters
- **In report:** 1 full page (high-impact summary)

### 2.6 Prioritized 90-Day Action Roadmap
- **What we do:** Create a step-by-step roadmap for implementing the 5 opportunities
- **Deliverable:** Month-by-month breakdown:
  - **Month 1 (Week 1-4):** Launch reactivation campaign; segment for upsell; build retention playbook
  - **Month 2 (Week 5-8):** Scale winner campaigns; launch cross-sell tests; prepare referral program
  - **Month 3 (Week 9-12):** Optimize; scale; measure; prepare next quarter
- **For each milestone:** Owner, success metric, expected revenue impact, dependencies
- **In report:** 2-3 pages; roadmap formatted as a Gantt-style visual + narrative

### 2.7 Executive PDF Report (15-20 Pages)
- **Contents:**
  1. Executive Summary (1 page): Problem, findings, top 3 opportunities, revenue potential, next steps
  2. Customer Database Overview (2 pages): Size, health, key metrics, data quality scorecard

  7. Appendix (2 pages): Methodology, data quality issues, recommendations for data cleanup
- **Format:** Professional, branded with Givra Tech logo; tables, charts, clear typography
- **Tone:** Data-driven, confident, actionable (not boring)

### 2.8 Excel with Segmented Customer List

## 5. Data Required From Client (Exact Specs)

### Core Data Files (Required)

**File 1: Customer Master List**
- Format: CSV or Excel
- Fields required: Customer ID, First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone (optional), City (optional), Country (optional)
- # of rows: All active customers in your system
- Notes: One row per customer; no duplicates

**File 2: Transaction History**
- Format: CSV or Excel
- Fields required: Transaction ID, Customer ID, Transaction Date (YYYY-MM-DD), Revenue ($), Product Category (optional)
- # of rows: All transactions in past 24 months minimum (ideally 36 months)
- Notes: One row per transaction; include returns/refunds as negative revenue

**File 3: Customer Engagement (Optional but Recommended)**
- Format: CSV or Excel
- Fields: Customer ID, Last Email Open Date, Last Click Date, # Emails Sent (past 12 months), # Emails Opened
- Notes: If you use email platform like Klaviyo/Mailchimp; helps us identify engagement decline

### File Submission

- **How to send:** Upload to shared Google Drive folder (link provided) or via email (encrypted link if large)
- **Naming convention:** `[Your Company]_[Data Type]_[Date]` (e.g., `BrandX_Customers_2026-03-30.csv`)
- **File size:** Should be fine with files <500MB each; if larger, compress or split by date range
- **Deadline:** Day 1; we start analysis as soon as received

### Data Quality Expectations

- **Acceptable:** 80%+ data completeness; some messy addresses/names
- **Need cleanup:** Email addresses 50% incomplete; duplicate customers; revenue negative/zero across most records
- **We'll handle:** Deduplication, data type matching, merging files across dates
